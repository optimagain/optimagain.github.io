5 Evening Habits That Shape How You Feel in the Morning

What happens between dinner and bed often matters more than what you ate at lunch. Here are three of the five worth changing tonight.

  1. Sitting straight down after dinnerTen to fifteen minutes of walking after your evening meal is one of the highest-return habits available. Working muscle uses glucose directly, and the effect is largest after the day's biggest meal.
  2. Eating late, then going to bedFinishing dinner within about three hours of sleep gives digestion time to settle before your body switches into overnight repair mode.
  3. The carbohydrate-only evening snackCrackers, biscuits or cereal alone at 9pm behave very differently from the same carbohydrate paired with protein or fat. Pairing changes how quickly it is absorbed.

Most people optimise the wrong end of the day

Almost all the attention goes on breakfast and lunch. But the stretch between dinner and sleep is long, largely sedentary, and sits right before the hours your body spends on repair.

It is also the easiest part of the day to change, because it requires no meal planning and no shopping. Just a different sequence of the things you already do.

Sleep is doing more than you think

Short or broken sleep affects how the body handles glucose the following day — the effect is measurable after a single poor night.

This is why people who eat carefully but sleep badly often plateau. Food is one input among several, and after 40 the others stop being optional.
