Privacy Policy

Last updated: . This explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Wellness After 40 is an independent publisher of educational health and wellness content for adults over 40. This site is operated at optimagain.net.

What we collect

Information you give us

If you request a free printable, we collect your email address and, optionally, your first name. We also record which guide you requested and which link brought you here, so we can send you the right file and understand which content is useful.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location by country, device type, and how you arrived. This is aggregated and we do not use it to identify individuals.

How we use it

  • To deliver the free guide you requested
  • To send a short educational email series, and occasional updates afterwards
  • To understand which content is helpful so we can make more of it

We do not sell your information. We do not share your email address with third parties for their own marketing.

Email

Our email is managed through GetResponse. Every email includes an unsubscribe link that works immediately. You can also reply to any email and ask us to remove you, and we will.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it entirely. Contact us and we will action it. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics only. We do not run advertising cookies or share your browsing data with advertising networks. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you buy through one of them, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. These links are disclosed on the pages where they appear. We only link to products we have researched, and a commission never changes what we write.

Medical disclaimer

Nothing on this site is medical advice. It is educational content and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult your physician or another qualified health provider about a medical condition, and before changing your diet, exercise or supplement routine.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Reply to any email you receive from us, or reach us through the contact details on our home page.
